02 / RESEARCH EXPERIENCE

Research, in practice.

JUL – AUG 2026

KIGAM

AI Research Intern
Resource Recovery Research Center

Offline tools for scientific decisions.

At the Korea Institute of Geoscience and Mineral Resources, I developed two offline applications that connected scientific inputs, predictive models, validation, and usable reports.

Lithium-recovery decision support

A Python pipeline and browser interface for prediction, optimization, validation, and PDF reporting. A treatment-aware, sign-constrained logit-linear surrogate used 128 runs compiled from published experiments, with leave-one-paper-out validation.

Logit-space prediction intervals achieved 96% empirical coverage at a nominal 95%. Local Ollama assistance supported explanations, schema mapping, and reports. Prompt splitting, prefix caching, and a resident model reduced time to first token from 23.2 to 1.2 seconds in the documented setup.

Spectrometry analysis automation

A 5,500-line Python package parsed vendor printouts and raw exports, applied six wavelength-selection criteria and EPA 6010D checks, and produced auditable multi-sheet Excel reports. Fourteen of eighteen elements matched the reference exactly.

The two systems included 64 and 135 tests, respectively, and offline installers for Windows and macOS. This account uses public CV descriptions only.
